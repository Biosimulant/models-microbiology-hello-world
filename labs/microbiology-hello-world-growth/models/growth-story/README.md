# Growth Story Reporter

This model is the final step in the hello-world lab. It reads the setup and growth results, then writes a friendly explanation for non-technical users.

## Inputs

- `growth_setup`: starting plate information.
- `colony_state`: final colony size, food, and limiting factor.
- `lesson_summary`: growth-model explanation.

## Outputs

- `hello_world_story`: what happened during the run.
- `next_steps`: simple ideas for the next experiment.
- `run_metadata`: status for the story step.
